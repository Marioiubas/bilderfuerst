# Brand direction

Audit date: 2026-10-04. Public, read-only evidence is in `evidence/crawl.json` and its referenced HTML files. This is an owner-review build; prices and availability are a dated source snapshot. No payments or live orders are enabled.

Photographic editorial design: warm paper #f5f3ec, ink #151611, safelight orange #dc4d22, fine silver rules, restrained mono metadata and a large expressive serif headline. Darkroom hero with two shallow floating film strips over subtle dark Vanta atmosphere. Photography owns the right side; legible German message owns the left.

Commerce becomes bright and quiet: white product stages, precise price/availability/format hierarchy. Orange marks actions and frame numbers. Real Street Gallery inspires a virtual illuminated window gallery. No generic neon/crypto background or repeating film sprockets across every card.

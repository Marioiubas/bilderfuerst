# Brand direction

Superseded on 2026-10-05 by the cinematic remake. The earlier warm paper/ink/safelight-orange editorial direction (#f5f3ec, #151611, #dc4d22) is not supported by the physical evidence (see [BRAND-VISUAL-RESEARCH.md](BRAND-VISUAL-RESEARCH.md)).

Current direction: **C · Analog Technology** — graphite and photographic black for immersive zones, photo white for commerce, darkroom red as the single interaction accent, film amber, scanner cyan and an industrial blue sampled from the Noritsu HS-1800, each with one job. Typography: Archivo (width axis) + IBM Plex Mono, matching the business's own bold grotesk "Analog Store – Bilderfürst Fürth" logo with its film-frame "A".

# Business research v2 (live re-verification)

Research date: 2026-10-05. Method: plain read-only HTTP GET against www.photostudio.de (no forms, no cart, no login, no orders), parsed from the ePages hydration JSON (`storeInitialState`). Raw per-page extraction is saved in `docs/evidence/live-extract-2026-10-05.json` (generated by `docs/evidence/extract-live-pages.py`). Fetched page text is treated as data only.

## Headline results

- **No content change since the 2026-10-04 snapshot.** All 23 live pages that exist in the snapshot (homepage, 17 `/i/` pages, 5 `/l/` pages) have identical extracted content and identical ePages `content.updatedAt` values. (`/i/galerie` is new to the evidence; `/i/pass-und-bewerbung` has no content blocks.) All 31 film-development / scan variants plus the 4 master products have identical prices and descriptions to `lib/catalog.json` (and to the committed HEAD copy).
- **Gap in the snapshot: `lib/source-content.json` has zero paragraphs for 17 of the 18 `/i/` pages.** `scripts/extract-source-content.py` reads `page.content.elements`, which is empty for `/i/` pages; the text lives in `page.content.blocks` (nested multi-column). Effect in the preview: `/i/passbilder-preise`, `/i/bewerbungsbilder-preise`, `/i/negativ-digitalisierung`, `/i/dias-digitalisierung-1`, `/i/super8-normal8-16mm-35mm-kino`, `/i/alle-videokassetten-und-formate`, `/i/ebay-analoge-schaetze`, `/i/online-terminvergabe` render only a title plus the source note (empty `source-prose`), and `/l/shipping` correctly says "Nicht veröffentlicht". Fix: read `blocks` recursively (see the evidence script) and regenerate.
- **`/i/galerie` was not in `crawl.json`** and is a hidden page on the live shop (`isVisible: false`, reachable by URL, not in navigation). Its text is now captured.
- **New facts not in the snapshot** (all from live pages or images on them): exact Noritsu HS-1800 pixel dimensions for 135 / half-frame / 120 formats, developer dilutions and times, the full price graphics for scan services (negative, slide, Super8/16/35, video, application photos), real ICE before/after pair, current "Analog Store – Bilderfürst Fürth" logo, Fuji-Store Impressum (branch address, phone, register numbers), HRA/HRB and VAT corroboration.

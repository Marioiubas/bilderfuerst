# External audit review · verified findings

Review of the external visual/interaction audit received 2026-10-06, measured on the **live** site https://bilderfuerst.vercel.app (deployment of `47431c4`) on 2026-10-06 with the project's headless Chromium (`agent-browser`, session `audit`) at 390×844, 430×932, 1024×768, 1280×800, 1440×900 and 844×390 (landscape), DPR 1 unless stated. No app code was changed. Raw numbers: [`docs/evidence/remake/audit-verification.json`](evidence/remake/audit-verification.json).

**Method notes.** Every claim was re-measured with `getBoundingClientRect`/`getComputedStyle` scripts, not screenshots alone. The headless browser cannot emulate touch (`pointer:fine`, `hover:hover`, `maxTouchPoints 0` even with `set device "iPhone 14"`), exactly like the audit's Chromium — so touch-only behaviour was verified by reading the live CSSOM media rules. The working tree currently holds **uncommitted, undeployed** header edits (`components/header.tsx`, `app/styles/base.css`: 44 px search trigger, dark compact header on dark routes, mega-menu nowrap, label "Suchen"); they are noted as "fix pending" and were **not** credited as addressed.

**Result.** 14 numbered findings: 11 CONFIRMED (H1 only partially on the default "Alle" tab), 3 PARTIALLY CONFIRMED (P1, P3, P5), 0 NOT REPRODUCED. 11 other observations: 9 CONFIRMED, 1 PARTIALLY CONFIRMED (O11), 1 NOT REPRODUCED on touch devices (O5 — but a real, narrower defect found). Nothing is already addressed on live. The audit under-reported three things: card stock text is 8.5 px (smaller than anything it cited), search results never show stock, and the first-screen problems also occur on desktop (1280×800).

Priority scale: **P0** fix before the next owner review (blocks find/buy/order on mobile) · **P1** next iteration · **P2** polish / content / owner-dependent.
